Standing rules for everyone

Three rules that don't bend.

1 · No unverified record ships

Every published record passes human SCRUB. If the timeline squeezes, cut features or record count — never the verification. It's the whole product.

2 · No claim without a quote + timestamp

Any number, model, or architecture detail carries its verbatim source. Absent = not_disclosed, shown as such. The chatbot answers only from the verified corpus, with citations.

3 · The date is protected by scope, not heroics

Cut order if slipping: stack-fallback → chatbot-to-grounded-search → fewer records → never the scrub. Raise a blocker the day you see it, not the week it's due.
