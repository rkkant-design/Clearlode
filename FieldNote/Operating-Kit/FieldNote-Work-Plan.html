Two-week work plan · Customer Support
Stage 1 → gate for Stage 2 · by Trueinfo Labs

Produce the records. Grade the evidence. Don't build software.

The job for the next two weeks is evidence, not engineering. Layer-1 (public sources) is enough to launch — interviews are optional. Everything ladders to one target.

The fortnight target

~20–25 editorial-grade records · a real measured cost-per-record · right-of-reply sent on every named-company record. Interviews are opportunistic, not required. Hit this and Stage 1 is done and the build-gate opens. Miss it and we diagnose before writing a line of product code.

Cadence
  • Daily, 15 min: each analyst — records done, blockers, time/record
  • Twice weekly: editor reviews the queue & signs off
  • End of week 1: gut-check — is a record economically producible?
  • End of week 2: gate review against the target
Track these numbers
  • Records finished / week / analyst
  • Minutes per record (the economics)
  • Grade mix (aim for real B's, not all C)
  • Right-of-reply sent / received
Do NOT yet
  • Build the app, AWS, or search — gate unmet
  • Scrape at scale (rights + quality)
  • Build a marketing site
  • Chase breadth over verified quality
Founder — don't delegate (yet)

The editorial standard for the first ~25 records, and the design-partner relationships. Delegate the labour, not the judgment or the relationships.

Day-one handout for every analyst

The Starter Kit (template + method + taxonomy + rights) and the Batch-1 records (Klarna · Commonwealth Bank · Intercom Fin) as the reference standard, so "good" is unambiguous.
