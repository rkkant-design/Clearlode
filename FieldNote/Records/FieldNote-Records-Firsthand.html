Record 01 · win + walk-back
Fintech (BNPL) · Global · Full production → hybrid (reversed)
Klarna — OpenAI-powered customer-service assistant
Tier-1 deflectionOrder / refund / statusLLM assistant
What it is
An OpenAI-powered assistant handling front-line customer chats (order status, refunds, cancellations, disputes), launched globally Feb 2024.
Reported outcomes
2.3M conversations in month one ≈ two-thirds of all support chats, "equivalent to 700 full-time agents"; average resolution time 11 min → under 2 min; repeat inquiries −25%; projected ~$40M profit improvement in 2024.Company-reported (Klarna press, 27 Feb 2024) · Grade B · self-reported, no independent baseline; $40M is a projection
Claim vs. reality (2025): Klarna reversed course. CEO Siemiatkowski: "we went too far… we focused too much on cost, the result was lower quality." Reported rising complaints and lower satisfaction on complex issues. New model = an "Uber-style" pool of remote human agents + AI tools; AI still handles ~two-thirds (~1.3M/mo).Firsthand — CEO Siemiatkowski on camera + Klarna's own press · Grade B
Challenge
Cost-first automation degraded quality on nuanced/complex cases; customers wanted a human path. Volume ≠ quality.
Lesson
Aggressive tier-1 automation can deliver large, fast volume and cost wins — but pushing it into complex/edge cases erodes quality and can force a public reversal. Condition: strong fit for high-volume, simple, repetitive contacts; keep an easy human path for complex ones. The stable end-state was hybrid, not full automation.
Boundaries & gaps
Feb-2024 metrics are first-party with no independent baseline; the "two-thirds" figure is volume, not quality; post-reversal quality/cost figures not disclosed.

Record 02 · firsthand, at scale
Fintech (neobank) · US · Full production
Chime — "Jade" AI support agent
Tier-1 deflectionAgent-assistFintech
What it is
Chime's AI support agent, "Jade," fronts customer support at scale (~50M support interactions/year). Described firsthand by the company's COO in a recorded interview.
Reported outcomes
Jade handles ~70% of interactions; resolution up >40 percentage points.Firsthand — Janelle Sallenave, COO (ex-Head of CX), on Fintech Wrap Up (video) · Grade B · single firsthand source; exact quote + timestamp to confirm on stream
The design principle: "Our golden rule: we will never introduce friction to reach a human." The COO says trust — not automation rate — was the hard part for financial cash-flow queries.Firsthand quote — confirm verbatim + timestamp on stream
Lesson
In high-trust financial support, automate volume but never gate the human path — the guaranteed escape hatch is what makes aggressive automation safe. Condition: high-stakes / regulated support where a wrong automated answer is costly.
Boundaries & gaps
Single firsthand source; exact quotes and timestamps pending the scrub; the baseline behind "+40pp" and the metric definition (resolution vs deflection) to be confirmed from the video.

Record 03 · the candid lesson
Commerce SaaS · Global · Full production
Lightspeed — Fin deflection + copilot agent-assist
Tier-1 deflectionAgent-assistSaaS
What it is
Lightspeed runs an AI agent for deflection plus an agent-assist copilot across a ~250-person global support org. Described firsthand by its VP of Global Support in a recorded talk.
Reported outcomes
Up to 65% AI resolution; the copilot let agents close +31% conversations per day.Firsthand — Angelo Livanos, VP Global Support, on Intercom's Innovator Spotlight (video) · Grade A · clear, specific, on-camera · quote + timestamp to confirm on stream
The candid lesson: the hard part was not the technology — it was AI change-management across a 250-person org: getting agents to trust and adopt the tools.Firsthand — the operator's own framing
Lesson
Budget for the organisational and change work, not just the tooling — adoption by the support team is the real bottleneck, and it's where most of the effort goes. Condition: mid-to-large support orgs with established agent teams.
Boundaries & gaps
Single firsthand source; baselines behind "65%" and "+31%" to confirm; vendor-hosted setting (Intercom Pioneer) — but the speaker is Lightspeed's own operator, so it passes the firsthand test.
