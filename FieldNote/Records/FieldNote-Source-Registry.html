Standing rules

How to keep it cheap and clean.

  • Firsthand rule applies within a channel — a session where the customer's own operator speaks is in; one where a vendor narrates the customer is out.
  • Crawl channels/playlists, not search — listing is ~1 API unit; search is ~100. Protect the daily 10,000-unit quota.
  • Fetch free captions first — only fall back to paid transcription when captions are disabled.
  • Log hit-rate per source (published ÷ ingested) and re-tier quarterly — promote what pays off, demote what doesn't.
  • Never publish without the SCRUB — the gate finds candidates; a human still confirms every quote and grade.
